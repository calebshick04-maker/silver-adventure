## Slide 3 — original 3D concept animation

`sprayer-in-action.mp4` is an original Blender-rendered animation, not footage of a built prototype. It shows a tractor towing a tank trailer with cameras and three vertically stacked fixed zones on one side. The tractor travels forward while the valves switch states at illustrative canopy gaps. Cyan droplets indicate illustrative spray, not simulated fluid dynamics or validated deposition.

The density values and controller states are synthetic explanatory inputs. They are not live AI inference, measured leaf-area density, actual pressure measurements or observed savings. The HUD labels this explicitly. The animation, still image and editable `.blend` scene are included. The fixed layout, dimensions and tank capacity need confirmation from the team's actual hardware.
